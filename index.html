Hillary Widanama Adare

Agri-tech entrepreneur and geospatial analyst applying satellite remote sensing, drones and machine learning to precision agriculture for smallholder farmers.

Founder & CEO, Wonfliki Mandela Washington Fellow, 2023 Chevening Scholar, 2025 Based in the United Kingdom
01

Profile

Hillary founded Wonfliki in 2019 to bring drone-based remote sensing, geospatial analysis and predictive analytics to smallholder farmers — a segment most commercial precision-agriculture tools are not built for. Her work spans data pipeline design, computer vision, GIS platform development and partnership delivery with government bodies and financial institutions. She holds a BSc in Geomatic Engineering (KNUST) and is completing an MSc in Agricultural Technology and Innovation (Royal Agricultural University) and an MPhil in Geographic Information Systems (KNUST).

02

Core services

Mapping
Geospatial mapping of farmland for planning and ongoing monitoring.
Crop monitoring
Satellite and remote-sensing based monitoring, covering vegetation health (NDVI), soil moisture estimation, yield prediction and pest risk analysis.
Drone spraying
Precision application services delivered by drone.
Bespoke services
Including site suitability analysis using remote sensing and GIS.
Technical stack. Built on open-source satellite and geospatial datasets rather than proprietary data feeds. Computer vision and object detection tasks use YOLO (Ultralytics). The product does not currently integrate IoT sensors — all monitoring is derived from satellite and drone-based remote sensing.
